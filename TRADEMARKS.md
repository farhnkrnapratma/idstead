# Trademark policy

"Idstead", the Idstead wordmark and any Idstead logo are the marks of the Idstead project. All rights reserved.

The code license ([AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE)) covers the code only. Under its section 7(e) it grants no rights in these marks. This policy explains what you may do without asking.

## Allowed without permission

- **Refer to the project.** Say that you use, write about, or contribute to Idstead.
- **Run Idstead.** A deployment of unmodified Idstead, changed only through its configuration, admin settings and content, may keep the Idstead name in the interface and say "Powered by Idstead".
- **Describe a fork.** A modified version may say it is "based on Idstead" or "a fork of Idstead", provided that its own name is clearly different.

## Not allowed without permission

- Using the Idstead name or logos as the name or logo of a modified version, whether you distribute it or run it as a service.
- Names, logos or domains that are confusingly similar to Idstead.
- Suggesting that your product, service or site is official or endorsed by the Idstead project.
- Selling merchandise with the Idstead name or logos.

A modified version has to replace the name before it is distributed or offered as a service. The name appears in the site navigation, the footer, page titles and the product name in `packages/domain/src/copyright.ts`.

This policy does not limit uses that the law allows without permission.
